Topic 1 • Vocabulary

Travel Words: Look for the Context Clue!

Each travel word has a specific purpose. Find the clue in the sentence:

  • TRIP: Focuses on the destination/purpose + short duration (going and coming back).
    Clues: weekend trip, business trip, day trip, school trip.
  • JOURNEY: Focuses on the long travel time from A to B (usually by land).
    Clues: takes days, long distance driving, train journey.
  • VOYAGE: A very long journey by SEA (ship/ocean) or in SPACE.
    Clues: Titanic, Atlantic Ocean, spacecraft, across the ocean.
  • DEPART vs SAIL: Depart = formal verb meaning "to leave" (flights, trains, gates).
    Sail = verb meaning "to travel on water" in a boat or ship.
Topic 2 • Grammar Connection

Relative Pronouns: Check Before & After!

The word before the blank gives the clue, and the word after confirms it:

  • WHO: Used ONLY for PEOPLE + followed by a verb/action.
    Ex: The boy who is playing... (Careful: animals use WHICH/THAT!).
  • WHICH / THAT: Used for THINGS or ANIMALS.
    Ex: The dog which/that is barking... / The book which/that is mine.
  • WHERE: Used for PLACES where an action happens.
    Ex: The restaurant where we ate the best pizza.
  • ⚠️ WHOSE: Shows POSSESSION ("belongs to"). Always placed between the owner and the noun!
    Formula: [Person] + WHOSE + [Noun: eyes, mother, passport].
Topic 3 • Timeline

Past Perfect vs. Past Simple: Time Machine

When two events happened in the past, ask yourself: which one happened FIRST?

ACTION 1 (Happened FIRST - Earlier Past) ➔ PAST PERFECT
Formula: HAD + Past Participle (3rd column / -ed)
Ex: had left, had already started, had forgotten, had never flown.
ACTION 2 (Happened SECOND - Later Past) ➔ PAST SIMPLE
Formula: Verb + -ED or 2nd column (Irregular)
Ex: When we arrived (Action 2), the movie had already started (Action 1).
💡 TEST TIP: Time expressions like "By the time..." or "When..." usually introduce Action 2 (Past Simple)!
Topic 4 • Hypothetical World

Second Conditional: Imaginary Situations

Used for hypothetical, imaginary, or unlikely situations in the present/future:

The Golden Formula:
IF + Simple Past , WOULD + Base Verb (Infinitive)
  • 🚫 NEVER put "would" inside the IF-clause!
    Watch out for inverted sentences! Ex: She would travel more if she had free time.
  • 👑 Special Rule for Verb TO BE: In the Second Conditional, we use WERE for ALL subjects (I, he, she, it)!
    Ex: If I were the President, I would build more schools.

Teaching Guide

Interactive Review Lesson Plan (50 min)

Designed to engage 100% of 8th-grade students through active note-taking and voice-controlled gamification.

1

Clue Hunting & Notebook Insights (15 min)

Project Tab 1. Notebook Insights. Divide the class into 4 groups (Rows A, B, C, and D). Assign 1 topic card to each group to present the "test trap" in 1 minute. All students copy the 4 visual summary boxes into their notebooks as their official test study guide.

2

Cooperative Voice Challenge (25 min)

Switch to Tab 2. Voice Challenge. The whole class plays together as one team! Click "Spin Speaker" to pick who answers the round (e.g., Window Row, Back Row, Choral Response). Give them 15 seconds to check their notebooks before speaking into the microphone.

3

Positive Tension: Climb & Drop Rule (10 min)

Because a wrong answer drops the class down 1 level and requires 5 new correct answers to climb back up, students naturally collaborate and peer-coach! Whenever feedback appears, ask the class to point out which clue word in the sentence gave away the answer.
